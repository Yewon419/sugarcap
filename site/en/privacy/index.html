SugarCap Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

SugarCap (the "app") has no account and no server. It does not collect identity information such as your name or contact details, and it sends usage data only if you consent.

1. What we don't collect

  • Identity information such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts or photos
  • Advertising identifiers or crash reports
  • Names of manually entered drinks, the exact time a drink was logged, or the sugar or caffeine amount of individual records

The app contains no advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps. Usage data is sent only if you give the optional consent in section 2.

2. Usage data sent only with your consent

After you close your first day, the app asks once for your "Consent to Collect and Use Usage Data to Improve the Service." The data below is sent only if you agree; if you don't, nothing is sent. You can use every feature of the app exactly the same either way.

  • Purpose: improving app features, filling gaps in drink menu data, and usage statistics. It is not used for advertising or marketing and is not sold to third parties.
  • Data: app usage records, search terms that returned no results, drink records (menu, cup count, weekday, time of day), randomly generated identifiers, app version, OS version, language and region settings. Only search terms that found nothing are sent, up to 20 characters, and text that looks like a phone number or email address is not sent. The identifiers are hashed on the device before sending and are not device serial numbers or advertising identifiers. They are not attached to drink records.
  • Retention: 2 years from collection, then deleted without delay.
  • Processor and transfer abroad: storage and analysis are entrusted to TelemetryDeck GmbH (Von-der-Tann-Str. 54, 86159 Augsburg, Germany), which under its processing agreement protects the data at a level equal to or higher than this policy. Data is sent over an encrypted connection as you use the app, to German servers of Amazon Web Services and Hetzner and Netherlands servers of Microsoft Azure used by the processor. Your device's IP address reaches the processor's servers during transmission, but the processor states that it does not store it anywhere, in databases or log files.
  • Withdrawal: turn off "Share Usage Data" under Settings > Privacy in the app. Collection stops immediately, and unsent records left on the device are deleted. Data already sent contains no value that identifies you, so it cannot be singled out and deleted per user; after you withdraw, nothing new is collected.

The full consent text is available under Settings > Privacy in the app.

3. What stays on your device

The drinks you log, your daily limits, your day-closing history, and character affinity are stored only on your device. These records themselves are never sent anywhere, and the developer cannot see them. If you consent in section 2, only the items in that section are sent separately. Deleting the app deletes it too.

4. Network use

The app uses the network to download the file of cafe menu sugar and caffeine values, to check the App Store for a new version, and, if you consent in section 2, to send usage data. Your entries are not sent when downloading the menu file or checking for a new version.

5. Purchases

SugarCap Pro purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment details, and the app has no access to them. The app only checks on your device whether you have purchased.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect identity information such as names or contact details from anyone.

7. Where the numbers come from

The sugar and caffeine values for cafe menus are taken from what each brand publishes on its official website or nutrition guide. Cup sizes published only in fluid ounces are converted to milliliters; the nutrition values themselves are not changed. Values a brand does not publish are shown as "Not published" and are never estimated. These values are for reference only and are not medical advice.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post it on this page with the date of the change.
