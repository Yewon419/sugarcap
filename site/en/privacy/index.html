SugarCap Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

SugarCap (the "app") does not collect personal information. There is no account and no server.

  2. 2. What stays on your device

1. What we don't collect

  • Identity information such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts, photos, or health data
  • Advertising identifiers, usage analytics, or crash reports

The app contains no analytics tools and no advertising SDKs.

2. What stays on your device

The drinks you log, your daily limits, your day-closing history, and character affinity are stored only on your device. This data is never sent anywhere, and the developer cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it too.

3. Network use

The app uses the network only to download the file of cafe menu sugar and caffeine values. Your entries are never sent.

4. Purchases

SugarCap Pro purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment details, and the app has no access to them. The app only checks on your device whether you have purchased.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone.

6. Where the numbers come from

The sugar and caffeine values for cafe menus are taken from what each brand publishes on its official website or nutrition guide. Cup sizes published only in fluid ounces are converted to milliliters; the nutrition values themselves are not changed. Values a brand does not publish are shown as "Not published" and are never estimated. These values are for reference only and are not medical advice.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post it on this page with the date of the change.
